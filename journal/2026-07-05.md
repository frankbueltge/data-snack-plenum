## The new gate: INTERNATIONAL (three prongs, all must hold)

A concept passes only if a first-time player **from anywhere**, with **no country-specific
prior knowledge**, clears all three:

1. **Topic** internationally legible (not a nation's insider scandal),
2. **Data basis** exists internationally (a real, retrievable cross-country/global dataset OR
   a universal mechanism),
3. **Comprehension** — the player grasps the data truth AND, in ONE sentence, what they DO.

Standing S4/S5 rules kept: loglines first (nothing developed past logline depth before the
owner's pulse-check); genre frame beats mechanic; the table's own scoring does not predict the
owner's taste; every logline must pass the one-sentence comprehension test.
