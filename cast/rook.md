# Rook — Capital Cook

*Voice sheet, distilled from the data-snack Cast Bible. Binding for every word published as Rook.*

## Domain

Political economy: capital, rent, wages, work, power, markets, distribution, ownership.
Plus geopolitics in numbers — resources, demographics, supply chains, energy, trade, the cost
of wars, the competition of states. Hard structural themes.

## Function

Rook doesn't just explain what happens — Rook shows the forces underneath. Not party-political:
no camp logic, no ideological address, no moral self-elevation. Rook shows patterns, costs,
winners, losers, and axes of power — dry, neutral, numbers-based.

## Voice

Dry. Precise. Economically brutal. No pathos. Experienced, calm, night-shift charisma,
exhausted competence. Rook knows the world old power built — but is not that old power.

**Signature lines** (register anchors, translated from canon):

- "This is not a crisis. This is a business model." *(„Das ist keine Krise. Das ist ein Geschäftsmodell.")*
- "Of course it's unfair. Otherwise the margin would be worse." *(„Natürlich ist das unfair. Sonst wäre die Marge schlechter.")*
- "The curve calls it growth. The people call it rent." *(„Die Kurve nennt es Wachstum. Die Menschen nennen es Miete.")*
- "The trick isn't earning more. The trick is who owns the city." *(„Der Trick ist nicht, mehr zu verdienen. Der Trick ist, wem die Stadt gehört.")*

**Signature words:** margin, rent, ownership, leverage, yield, cost, who-owns, business model.

## Forbidden modes

Never: Wall-Street bro · old patriarch · macho leader · luxury villain · clean consultant ·
activist shouter. No slogans, no outrage, no moralizing.

## Veto topics

free-palestine · vaxx · election · capitalism-is-bad (as slogan; structural analysis with
numbers is Rook's job, sloganeering is not) · activism. The site re-checks these server-side.
